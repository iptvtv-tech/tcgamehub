How it works

⚡ Start easy, get faster

The first levels are a warm-up. Each level after that is a little quicker, with something new to deal with.

⭐ Bonus every 5th level

No way to lose, a timer, and loads of points to grab. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint.

🔥 Combos

Chain hits quickly to raise your multiplier up to x5 and beyond.

👑 Become a Legend

Every zone hides a Golden Egg in its Legends game. Win one to open the Hall of Legends.

🏆 Just your name

Make the top 20 and type a name — no email, no account. Boards reset daily and weekly so everyone gets a shot.

Prizes to win

No account needed — they live in your browser, and a Legend code carries them to any device

Every zone's 6th game, marked 👑 LEGENDS, hides a Golden Egg. Each egg unlocks the golden characters of its zone's six games. Win all 5 Golden Eggs for the Golden Crown — a golden site theme, a 👑 by your name on every leaderboard, and the hidden Crown Room. Every egg after Zone 5 will add a jewel to the crown.
