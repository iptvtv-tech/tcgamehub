About the arcade

Short, free games you can play in any modern browser, on a computer, tablet or phone. No account, no download, no email address.

How scoring works

  • Levels: each level is a little faster (about 6–7% per level) and every few levels adds something new.
  • Bonus rounds: every 5th level. You can't lose, there's a countdown, and the points are big.
  • Checkpoints: once you've reached level 6, 11, 16… you can start from there next time.
  • Combos: chain hits to raise your multiplier. It resets if you pause too long or miss.
  • Daily Challenge: one game each day uses the same level layout for everyone, with its own board. It changes at midnight UTC.

Fair play

Scores are checked when they're submitted. Impossible scores, very short games and rapid-fire submissions are rejected, and rude names are blocked. If you see a name that is rude, hurtful or uses someone's real name without their permission, email us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll remove it.

Health warning: flashing lights

Some games use brief screen flashes and fast-moving colours. We keep flashes soft and limit how often they happen, and they switch off completely if your device's "reduce motion" setting is on. If you or anyone playing has photosensitive epilepsy, please take care, and stop playing if you feel unwell.

What we store, and why.

  • High scores you choose to save: the nickname you type, your score, the level reached, the game and the time. These are shown publicly on the leaderboards. We keep them because running a public leaderboard is the point of the site (legitimate interests). Scores older than 90 days are deleted unless they are in a game's all-time top 20.
  • Hall of Legends: if you finish a Legend game and choose to sign the Hall, we store the nickname you type, the game, the date and a private restore code. Names in the Hall are kept until you ask us to remove them.
  • Spam protection: when you save a score, a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your connection (IP) address is stored briefly to limit spam, and deleted automatically, normally within an hour. It is never shown or shared.
  • On your own device: your best scores, badges, game progress and sound settings are saved in your browser's local storage so the games remember them. This data never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it. We don't use cookies, advertising or tracking.

Please use a nickname. Don't enter your full real name or anyone else's.

Services we use: the site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives your IP address like any web host does in order to deliver the pages, and the leaderboard is stored with Supabase. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK/EU under approved safeguards.
