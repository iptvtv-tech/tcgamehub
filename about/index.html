About the arcade

Short, free games you can play in any modern browser, on a computer, tablet or phone. No account, no download, no email address.

How scoring works

  • Levels: each level is a little faster (about 6–7% per level) and every few levels adds something new.
  • Bonus rounds: every 5th level. You can't lose, there's a countdown, and the points are big.
  • Checkpoints: once you've reached level 6, 11, 16… you can start from there next time.
  • Combos: chain hits to raise your multiplier. It resets if you pause too long or miss.
  • Daily Challenge: one game each day uses the same level layout for everyone, with its own board. It changes at midnight UTC.

Fair play

Scores are checked when they're submitted — impossible scores, too-short games and rapid-fire submissions are rejected, and rude names are blocked. If you spot a name that shouldn't be there, let us know and we'll remove it.

Privacy

When you save a high score we store only the name you type, your score, the level reached, the game and the time. We don't use cookies, accounts or advertising trackers.

To stop spam, a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your connection address is kept for up to one hour and then deleted automatically. It is never shown or shared.

Your personal bests, badges and settings are saved only in your own browser (local storage). Clearing your browser data removes them.

Don't use your full real name if you'd rather not — any nickname up to 12 letters and numbers works.

Credits
