TC Pixel Craft 3D

Block building survival game — An open-world block survival game in your browser. Start with nothing, punch trees, craft tools, find food, survive the nights and face the Shadow King.

3D adventure Survival 3D Ultra · Zone 4 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play TC Pixel Craft 3D

  1. Move with WASD, look with the mouse (click the view first), Shift to sprint.
  2. Left click mines or hits; right click places a block, uses an item or eats.
  3. Press E for your bag and the crafting menu.
  4. Your energy drops over time — find food before night falls.

Controls: WASD move · Shift sprint · Mouse look (click the view) · Left click mine/hit · Right click place/use/eat · E bag & crafting · L advancements (touch: stick, drag to look, HIT / USE / ⤒).

💡 Advancements are earned as you play; press L to see them.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

TC Pixel Craft 3D is game 19 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 4 · Ultra (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's TC Pixel Craft 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crown Heist 3D, the zone's Legends game.
