Crown Heist 3D

3D stealth game — Sneak into the Deep Vaults by torchlight, lift the crown from its pedestal and run. Fifteen floors of guards, portcullises and crumbling floors. Zone 4’s Legends game.

Stealth 3D adventure 3D Ultra · Zone 4 👑 Legends game Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Crown Heist 3D

  1. Move with ↑ ↓ (W S), turn with ← → (A D), sidestep with Q and E; on a phone use the stick and drag the view.
  2. Stay out of the guards’ torch cones — hide in alcoves and throw coins (C) to lure them away.
  3. Grab the crown (Space), then run for the stairs before the vault seals.
  4. Smoke bombs (X) lose a guard that is chasing you.

Controls: Move: ↑/↓ or W/S · Turn: ←/→ or A/D · Sidestep: Q/E · Grab / lever: Space · Coin: C · Smoke: X (touch: stick, drag the view to look, GRAB / COIN / SMOKE).

💡 Carry the crown out of floor 15 — over as many visits as it takes — to win the Golden Throne Egg.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Crown Heist 3D is game 24 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 4 · Ultra (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Crown Heist 3D challenge.

The Legends secret

Crown Heist 3D is Zone 4 · Ultra's Legends game. Carry the crown out of all 15 floors of Crown Heist (over as many visits as it takes). Win the egg to enter the Hall of Legends — and the Golden Eggs of Zones 1–4 together win the Golden Crown.

“You saw the crown in the Crypt. Now steal it from the Vault.”

Badges in Crown Heist 3D

  • 👻 Ghost — Escape floor 3 or later without a single guard spotting you
  • ⛓️ Shortcut — Pull a lever to raise a slammed portcullis
  • 🪙 Heads or Tails — Lure a guard away with a thrown coin
  • 💨 Smoke and Mirrors — Lose a guard who is chasing you with a smoke bomb
  • 🕳️ Mind the Gap — A guard falls through a floor that gave way behind you
  • 👁️ Outran the Wraith — Escape a floor with the Crown Wraith awake
  • 💰 Vault Raider — Grab 40 coins in one treasure vault
  • 🏰 The Deep Vaults — Reach floor 10
  • 🔒 2 secret badges — keep playing…

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Crown Heist 3D unlocks a golden character: the Golden Thief.
