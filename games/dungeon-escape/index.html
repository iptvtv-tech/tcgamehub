Dungeon Escape 3D

3D maze game — Lost in a 3D maze with a dying torch. Grab the keys, find the stairs and stay a step ahead of the ghouls hunting you in the dark. Free first-person game.

3D adventure Action 3D Ultra · Zone 4 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Dungeon Escape 3D

  1. Walk with ↑ and ↓ (W and S), turn with ← and → (A and D); on a phone use the stick.
  2. Find every key, then the stairs, before your torch burns out.
  3. Press Space or ✨ to flash your torch and stun a ghoul in front of you.
  4. The minimap shows where you have been.

Controls: Walk: ↑/↓ or W/S · Turn: ←/→ or A/D · Flash: Space or ✨ (touch: tap sides to turn, middle to walk, bottom to step back).

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Dungeon Escape 3D is game 20 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 4 · Ultra (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Dungeon Escape 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crown Heist 3D, the zone's Legends game.

Badges in Dungeon Escape 3D

  • 🛡️ Untouched — Escape dungeon 3+ without losing a life
  • 🤫 Silent Runner — Escape dungeon 3+ without using your flash
  • ⚡ Sprinter — Escape with more than half your torch left
  • 💰 Dragon Hoard — Grab 100 coins in the Treasure Vault
  • 🔥 Hot Streak — Reach a x4 combo
  • 🏰 Deep Delver — Reach dungeon 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Dungeon Escape 3D unlocks a golden character: the Golden Torch.
