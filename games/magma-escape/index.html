Magma Escape

Platform climbing game — Climb out of an erupting volcano as a man made of ice. The lava rises, every burn melts you smaller, and three fire bosses wait on the way up.

Action Arcade Ultra · Zone 6 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Magma Escape

  1. Climb from ledge to ledge up to the cold air of the vent at the top, before the rising lava catches you.
  2. Your size is your health: every burn melts you smaller, and a smaller Iceman jumps lower. Ice crystals freeze you back up.
  3. Slash the magma creatures that drip from above. A slash while falling is a jump-stab that bounces you up.
  4. Levels 5, 10 and 15 are boss fights: your blade only hurts a boss while it is dazed and glowing white.

Controls: ← → move · ↑ or Space jump (hold for higher) · X, J or click to slash (touch: ◀ ▶, JUMP and SLASH).

💡 Hold JUMP for a higher jump; a quick tap is a short hop that still clears a ledge.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Magma Escape is game 31 of 42 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 6 · Inferno (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Magma Escape challenge.

  • 💎 Every Crystal — Collect every ice crystal on a level
  • ❄️ Frozen Solid — Climb out of a level still at full size
  • 🔥 Halfway Up — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one game
