Turbo Rush 3D

3D racing game — Race the clock from the driver’s seat through coast roads, mountain passes, tunnels and city freeways. Near-miss combos, nitro, police and rival duels. Free.

Racing & driving 3D Master · Zone 3 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Turbo Rush 3D

  1. Steer with ← and → (A and D); on a phone drag the wheel or tilt.
  2. Reach each checkpoint before the clock runs out.
  3. Skim past cars for near-miss combos; drive clean and slipstream to charge nitro, then hold ↑ to use it.
  4. Press C to switch between the cockpit and the chase camera.

Controls: Steer ← → (or A / D) · hold ↑ / W for nitro · ↓ / S brake · C swaps cockpit / chase cam · tuck in behind a car to slipstream · touch: hold the left or right side to steer, the middle to boost, 🎥 VIEW to swap camera.

💡 Beat VIPER to the checkpoint in a rival duel for the Viper Slayer badge.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Turbo Rush 3D is game 13 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 3 · Master Class (Master difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Turbo Rush 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crypt of Crowns 3D, the zone's Legends game.

Badges in Turbo Rush 3D

  • 🧼 Clean Run — Clear level 3+ without crashing
  • 💨 Hair's Breadth — Chain 10 near misses in one level
  • 📸 Photo Finish — Reach a checkpoint with under 2 seconds left
  • 🪙 Coin Highway — Collect 60 coins in one bonus round
  • 🐍 Viper Slayer — Beat VIPER to the checkpoint in a rival duel
  • 🌬️ Slipstreamer — Pull off 5 slipstreams in one level
  • 🔥 On a Roll — Reach a x4 combo
  • 🏁 Road Warrior — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Turbo Rush 3D unlocks a golden character: the Golden Racer.
