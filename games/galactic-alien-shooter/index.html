Galactic Alien Shooter

Galaga style shooter — Fight five alien warships with five weapons, rescue your abducted ship for a twin fighter and take on four bosses. Free retro space shooter.

Shooter Space Extreme · Zone 2 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Galactic Alien Shooter

  1. Fly with the mouse, arrow keys, or drag on a phone.
  2. Hold Space or the mouse button to fire (touch and hold on a phone).
  3. Destroy the whole fleet to clear the level; some ships dive at you.
  4. Pick up lasers, rockets and blasters, and power each one up to level 3.

Controls: Fly: mouse / arrow keys / drag (up, down, left, right) · Fire: hold Space or mouse button (touch & hold on phones).

💡 Let a carrier abduct your ship, then shoot it down to fly a twin fighter.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Galactic Alien Shooter is game 11 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 2 · Hard Mode (Extreme difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Galactic Alien Shooter challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Heist Planner, the zone's Legends game.

Badges in Galactic Alien Shooter

  • 🚀 Twin Engines — Rescue your abducted ship for a twin fighter
  • 🛸 Wing Clipper — Shoot down a diving carrier and both escorts
  • 🔋 Fully Loaded — Power a weapon up to level 3
  • ☠️ Dreadnought Down — Destroy the Dreadnought boss
  • 🎯 Sharpshooter — Clear level 2+ with 75% accuracy
  • ✨ Perfect Run — Hit all 40 ships in a Star Run bonus
  • 🔥 On Fire — Reach a x4 combo
  • 🌌 Galaxy Defender — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Galactic Alien Shooter unlocks a golden character: the Golden Starfighter.
