How the arcade works

Games are grouped into zones of six, from Easy up to Ultra — the recommended order, though every game is open from the start. Every 5th level is a bonus round, the best scores go on a public board under a nickname, and the sixth game of each zone hides a Golden Egg for a flawless run. More about the arcade →
