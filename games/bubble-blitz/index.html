Bubble Blitz 3D

Bubble shooter — Fire glossy 3D bubbles up the table and pop groups of three. Sparkly bubbles hide bonuses or traps, and the ceiling keeps dropping. Free bubble shooter.

Puzzle Arcade 3D Master · Zone 3 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Bubble Blitz 3D

  1. Aim with the mouse and click to fire; on a phone drag to aim and let go.
  2. Match three or more of a colour to pop them.
  3. Clear the board before the ceiling reaches the line.
  4. Press ↑ or tap NEXT to swap the bubble you are holding.

Controls: Aim with the mouse and click to fire · touch: drag to aim, let go to fire · ← → aim, Space fire, ↑ swap (or tap NEXT).

💡 Bounce a shot off the wall to reach bubbles hiding behind others.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Bubble Blitz 3D is game 14 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 3 · Master Class (Master difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Bubble Blitz 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crypt of Crowns 3D, the zone's Legends game.
