Star Strike 3D

3D space shooter — Fly a starfighter down a 3D space corridor. Grab spread shot, plasma, homing missiles and a wingman drone, and take down three bosses. Free.

Shooter Space 3D Master · Zone 3 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Star Strike 3D

  1. Fly with the arrow keys, WASD or the mouse; drag on a phone.
  2. Hold Space or the mouse button to fire; on a phone you fire while touching.
  3. Press B or 💣 for a bomb that clears the screen.
  4. Fly into capsules to upgrade your weapons.

Controls: Fly: arrows / WASD or mouse (touch: drag) · Fire: hold Space / mouse (touch fires automatically) · Bomb: B or 💣 · fly into or shoot power-up capsules.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Star Strike 3D is game 16 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 3 · Master Class (Master difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Star Strike 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crypt of Crowns 3D, the zone's Legends game.
