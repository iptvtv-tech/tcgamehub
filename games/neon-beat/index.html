Neon Beat

Rhythm game — Hit the falling notes on the beat and every note you hit plays the tune. Chords, long holds and stealth notes. A free rhythm game in your browser.

Arcade Rhythm Expert · Zone 2 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Neon Beat

  1. Press ← ↓ ↑ → (or A S W D) as each note reaches the line, or tap the lanes on a phone.
  2. Hold the key for long notes.
  3. Perfect timing scores more than Good; a miss costs health.
  4. Finish the song to clear the level.

Controls: ← ↓ ↑ → or A S W D · tap the lanes on touch screens · hold for long notes.

💡 Press [ or ] on a keyboard to shift the timing if the notes feel early or late.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Neon Beat is game 8 of 42 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 2 · Hard Mode (Expert difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Neon Beat challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Heist Planner, the zone's Legends game.

Badges in Neon Beat

  • 🎯 Full Combo — Finish a song (level 2+) without a single miss
  • 💎 Pitch Perfect — Finish a song with nothing but PERFECTs
  • 🔥 In the Zone — Hit a 100-note streak
  • 🎹 Sustain — Complete 10 hold notes in one song
  • ⭐ Fever Pitch — Hit 60 notes in one Fever bonus
  • 🎧 Headliner — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Neon Beat unlocks a golden character: the Golden Notes.
