Inferno Vault

Stealth platform game — Sneak down through the Fire King's vault as a man made of ice. Stay out of the lantern light, hide in the dark, and put the fire guards out from behind.

Stealth Action Ultra · Zone 6 Legends game Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Inferno Vault

  1. Work your way down through the vault's floors to the vault door at the bottom of each level.
  2. Each fire guard carries a lantern. Its light melts you, and once a guard sees you it throws fireballs until it loses you again.
  3. Step into a dark alcove to hide: a guard can't see you there, and it walks right past.
  4. Slash a guard from behind to put it out. From the front your blade bounces off its flame, and it sees you.
  5. Your size is your health: lava, flames and lantern light melt you smaller. Frost shards freeze you back up. Your blade can smash a fireball out of the air.
  6. From level 6 you can freeze. It turns a lava pool into an ice bridge for a few seconds and a guard into an ice statue that one slash shatters. It also stops a flame jet, and with up held it puts out a heat lamp above you. Each freeze costs a little of your ice.
  7. Deep in the vault (levels 11 to 15) the vault door is locked until you find the ember key, which also opens the iron hatches. Cold vents freeze you back up. Brutes are too hot for your blade, so freeze them first, and a guard that sees you rings the alarm.
  8. Fire opals are the loot: take them all for a bonus. Reach the door without being seen for the Ghost bonus.

Controls: ← → move · ↑ or Space jump (hold for higher) · X, J or click to slash · F or L to freeze, from level 6 (↑ + freeze: above you) (touch: ◀ ▶, JUMP, SLASH and FREEZE).

💡 Watch a guard walk its round before you drop down to its floor: drop in just after it has walked past.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels and scoring

Inferno Vault is game 36 on TC Arcade (one of 33 free games), in Zone 6 · Inferno (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • 15 levels, each a stack of vault floors to work down through. The guards walk a little faster on every level, from level 6 there are lava pools too wide to jump, flame jets and swinging heat lamps, and from level 11 ember keys, brutes, sentinels and the alarm.
  • Points grow with the level: fire opals, guards put out and fireballs smashed, with a bonus at the vault door for the ice you have left and a big Ghost bonus if no guard saw you.
  • Reach level 6 or 11 and you can start from there next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.

The Legends secret

Inferno Vault is Zone 6 · Inferno's Legends game. Start at level 1 and reach the vault door of all 15 levels without losing a life. The heart of the vault opens only for an Iceman who never melts… Win the crystal to enter the Hall of Legends — and the 6 crystals of Zones 1–6 together win the Frost Crown.

“Legends say the Fire King keeps the Inferno Crystal at the very heart of his vault: fire, sealed in ice.”
