Astro Explorer

Space exploration platformer — Explore alien planets as a lone astronaut. Blade and blast the aliens, find new moves, upgrade your suit at rest beacons and beat a guardian boss on every fifth planet. Free.

Action Space Ultra · Zone 5 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Astro Explorer

  1. Run with A / D or the arrow keys and jump with Space or W. Hold jump to go higher.
  2. Swing the energy blade with J or X; hold ↑ to strike upwards. In mid-air, hold ↓ and swing to bounce off aliens and spikes.
  3. Blade hits charge the blaster (K or C). Stand still and hold ↓ to turn 3 energy into an armour plate.
  4. Each planet hides an ability capsule: double jump, then dash (L, Shift or V), then wall-cling. New moves open up secrets.
  5. Light the rest beacon to repair your suit and spend star dust on armour, blade and blaster upgrades. Every fifth planet is a guardian’s lair, then a treasure vault.
  6. On a phone, use the stick to run and aim, plus the JUMP, BLADE, BLAST and DASH buttons.

Controls: A / D or ← → run · Space / W jump · J or X blade · K or C blaster · L, Shift or V dash · ↑ ↓ aim · hold ↓ to heal · on touch: stick + JUMP, BLADE, BLAST, DASH.

💡 Cracked rock can be broken with the blade, and there’s always something behind it.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Astro Explorer is game 29 of 42 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 5 · Beyond (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Astro Explorer challenge.
