Neon Drift 3D

3D drift racing game — Neon racing from the driver’s seat on a glowing grid circuit. Throw the car sideways, chain drifts, dodge oil and beat five rivals — plus a boss every 5th level.

Racing & driving 3D Ultra · Zone 4 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Neon Drift 3D

  1. Steer with ← and → (A and D); on a phone drag the wheel or tilt.
  2. Press Space or ↓ to handbrake into a drift; hold it round the bend.
  3. Press ↑ (W) to fire the boost you charged while drifting.
  4. Beat the rivals to the flag to win the race.

Controls: Steer: ← → or A/D · Handbrake drift: Space or ↓ · Fire drift boost: ↑ or W · Cockpit / chase cam: C (phone: tilt to steer and hold the middle to drift, or hold the left or right side · tap ⚡ BOOST to fire · tap VIEW to switch camera).

💡 Full lock through a bend starts a drift on its own.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Neon Drift 3D is game 21 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 4 · Ultra (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Neon Drift 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crown Heist 3D, the zone's Legends game.
