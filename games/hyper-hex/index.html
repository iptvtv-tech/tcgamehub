Hyper Hex

Reflex arcade game — Circle the core and slip through the gaps as hexagon walls close in and the world spins. Pure reflexes. Free Super Hexagon style browser game.

Arcade Action Extreme · Zone 2 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Hyper Hex

  1. Hold ← or → (A or D) to circle; on a phone hold the left or right side of the screen.
  2. Slip through the gap in each ring of walls as it closes in.
  3. Survive the clock to clear the level; the spin and speed rise every level.
  4. Tap once on a phone to hop one lane.

Controls: Hold ← → or A / D · touch: hold the left or right side of the screen.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Hyper Hex is game 10 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 2 · Hard Mode (Extreme difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Hyper Hex challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Heist Planner, the zone's Legends game.

Badges in Hyper Hex

  • 🎯 Untouchable — Clear level 3+ without losing a life
  • ⏱️ Hyper Minute — Survive a 60-second level
  • 💎 Gem Rush — Grab 20 gems in one bonus round
  • 🔥 In the Flow — Reach a x4 combo
  • 🌀 Hexpert — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Hyper Hex unlocks a golden character: the Golden Arrow.
