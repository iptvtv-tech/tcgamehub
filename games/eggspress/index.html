Eggspress 3D

Egg delivery driving game — Deliver one very loose egg over the hills to the farm. Ramps, chasms, wind and oil — hard landings crack it, and three cracks is splat. Free physics game.

Racing & driving Action 3D Master · Zone 3 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Eggspress 3D

  1. Drive with → (D or Space) and brake or reverse with ← (A); on a phone hold the right or left side.
  2. In the air, → tips the truck back and ← tips it forward.
  3. Land gently — every hard landing cracks the egg.
  4. Reach the farm with the egg in its cup.

Controls: Drive: → / D / Space · Brake & reverse: ← / A · in the air → tips back, ← tips forward (touch: hold right side to drive, left side to brake).

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Eggspress 3D is game 17 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 3 · Master Class (Master difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Eggspress 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crypt of Crowns 3D, the zone's Legends game.

Badges in Eggspress 3D

  • 🦅 Big Air — Land a 1.5 second jump with the egg still in its cup
  • 🍄 Boing Boing — Bounce off a mushroom and land with the egg safe
  • 🌉 Mind the Gap — Jump a chasm with the egg safe
  • 🪙 Coin Collector — Grab 50 coins in one run
  • 🔥 Coin Streak — Reach a x4 combo
  • 🏁 Long Haul — Reach delivery 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run
