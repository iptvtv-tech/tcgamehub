Void Breaker 3D

3D arena shooter — Stand at the heart of a glowing arena as geometric invaders pour in from every side. Light-trail shots, a NOVA shockwave and a giant boss every 5th wave. Free.

Shooter Space Action 3D Ultra · Zone 4 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Void Breaker 3D

  1. Aim with the mouse (click to lock the cursor) or ← and →; drag to turn on a phone.
  2. Hold the mouse button, Space or your finger to fire.
  3. Press ↑ (W) or NOVA for a shockwave that clears the ring around you.
  4. Pick an upgrade between waves.

Controls: Aim: mouse (click to lock) or ← → · Fire: hold click / Space · Nova: ↑ / W · Turn round: ↓ / S (touch: drag to turn, hold to fire, NOVA and ⟲ buttons).

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Void Breaker 3D is game 23 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 4 · Ultra (Ultra difficulty). The zone opens once you have played every game in Zone 3.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Void Breaker 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crown Heist 3D, the zone's Legends game.
