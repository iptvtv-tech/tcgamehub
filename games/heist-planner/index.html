Heist Planner

Strategy puzzle game — Plan every step of your crew — thief, hacker and muscle — then press GO and watch the heist play out. Free turn-based puzzle. Zone 2’s Legends game.

Puzzle Stealth Extreme · Zone 2 👑 Legends game Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Heist Planner

  1. Pick a crew member and tap the tiles next to them (or use the arrows) to plan their moves.
  2. Space adds a wait, Tab switches crew, Enter or GO runs the plan.
  3. Guards and cameras follow fixed patterns — plan around them.
  4. Every crew member must reach the van with the loot.

Controls: Arrows / tap next to a crew member to plan steps · Space wait · Tab switch crew · Enter GO.

💡 Pull off a job with your very first plan for the Mastermind badge.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Heist Planner is game 12 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 2 · Hard Mode (Extreme difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Heist Planner challenge.

The Legends secret

Heist Planner is Zone 2 · Hard Mode's Legends game. Start at job 1 and don't lose a single life. A hidden door waits beyond job 15… Win the egg to enter the Hall of Legends — and the Golden Eggs of Zones 1–4 together win the Golden Crown.
