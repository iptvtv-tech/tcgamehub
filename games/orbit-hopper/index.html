Orbit Hopper

One-button space game — Circle a planet, let go at the right moment and slingshot through gravity to the next one. Climb to the golden beacon, grab stars on the way, and don’t drift into the void. Free.

Space Arcade Action Ultra · Zone 5 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Orbit Hopper

  1. Your hopper circles the planet on its own — watch the direction it is facing.
  2. Press Space, click or tap to let go. You fly straight, bent by every planet’s gravity.
  3. Touch a planet to swing into its orbit. Each new planet pays points and grows your combo.
  4. Reach the golden ★ beacon at the top to finish the level. From level 4 planets drift, and from level 7 a black hole lurks between them.

Controls: Space, click or tap: let go of the planet.

💡 A near miss is a free slingshot: grazing a planet’s gravity bends you around it and flings you out faster.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Orbit Hopper is game 26 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 5 · Beyond (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Orbit Hopper challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Vault of Stars, the zone's Legends game.
