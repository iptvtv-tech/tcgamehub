The Vault Job

Stealth puzzle game — Sneak through a museum after dark, one tile at a time. Dodge guards, cameras and lasers, grab the loot and reach the exit. Zone 1’s Legends game.

Stealth Puzzle Hard · Zone 1 👑 Legends game Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play The Vault Job

  1. Move one tile at a time with the arrow keys, WASD, or a swipe.
  2. Guards walk fixed routes; wait or hide behind plants and statues until they pass.
  3. Grab the treasure, then get to the exit before the lights come on.
  4. Lasers and cameras switch on and off on a timer — watch the pattern.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD / swipe to move one tile · hide behind plants and statues.

💡 Finish all 15 levels from level 1 without losing a life and something waits beyond level 15.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

The Vault Job is game 6 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 1 · Arcade Classics (Hard difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's The Vault Job challenge.

The Legends secret

The Vault Job is Zone 1 · Arcade Classics's Legends game. Start at level 1 and don't lose a single life. Something waits beyond level 15… Win the egg to enter the Hall of Legends — and the Golden Eggs of Zones 1–4 together win the Golden Crown.

“Legends say a flawless thief finds something special…”

Badges in The Vault Job

  • 🚪 Great Escape — Get out of the museum after level 15
  • 💰 Clean Sweep — Grab every treasure in a level
  • 🌿 In the Shadows — Stay hidden while a guard walks right past you
  • 👻 Ghost — Complete a level without triggering a single alarm
  • 🔦 Night Shift — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run
  • 🔒 1 secret badge — keep playing…

Win the zone's Golden Egg and The Vault Job unlocks a golden character: the Golden Thief.
