Pinball Blast 3D

3D pinball game — Neon 3D pinball against the clock with chaos events: ghost balls, earthquakes, magnets, reversed flippers, portals and black holes. Free online pinball.

Arcade Action 3D Master · Zone 3 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Pinball Blast 3D

  1. Flippers: ← and → (A and D), or hold the left or right half of the screen.
  2. Hold Space or your finger to pull the plunger and let go to launch.
  3. Complete the mission shown at the top before the clock runs out.
  4. Nudge with ↑ — too much and the table tilts.

Controls: Flippers: ← → or A / D (touch: hold left / right half) · Launch: hold Space / touch, let go · Nudge: ↑ (too much = TILT).

💡 Lock two balls to start multiball, then hit the UFO for the jackpot.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Pinball Blast 3D is game 15 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 3 · Master Class (Master difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Pinball Blast 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crypt of Crowns 3D, the zone's Legends game.
