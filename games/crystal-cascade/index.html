Crystal Cascade 3D

Match-3 puzzle shooter — Fire crystals onto a turning platform and match three to shatter them. Set off cascades, bombs and drills, and crack the gold geodes before the stack breaks the laser ring.

Puzzle Shooter 3D Ultra · Zone 4 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Crystal Cascade 3D

  1. Aim with the mouse or ← and →; on a phone drag to aim.
  2. Click, press Space or let go to fire.
  3. Match three crystals of a colour to shatter them; chains score more.
  4. Crack the ◆ geodes marked on the board to finish the level.

Controls: Aim: mouse / ← → (touch: drag) · Fire: click / Space (touch: let go) · hold fire during Fever · Swap: ↑ or tap NEXT.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Crystal Cascade 3D is game 22 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 4 · Ultra (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Crystal Cascade 3D challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Crown Heist 3D, the zone's Legends game.

  • 🪨 Rock Breaker — Break 15 obsidian blocks in one game
  • 🌀 Head Spin — Clear a reversing level (6+) without losing a life
  • 🌧️ Crystal Rain — Shatter 80 crystals in one bonus round
  • 🔥 Shard Storm — Reach a x5 combo
  • ⛏️ Deep Miner — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run
