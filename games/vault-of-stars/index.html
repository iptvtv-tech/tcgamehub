Vault of Stars

Speedrun puzzle game — Fifteen sealed vaults on an alien moon. Time the laser grids, slip past sentry drones, drift across zero-g fields and reach the airlock before your oxygen runs out. Zone 5’s Legends game.

Puzzle Space Ultra · Zone 5 👑 Legends game Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Vault of Stars

  1. Move one tile per step with the arrow keys, WASD, a swipe or the on-screen pad.
  2. Everything moves on the same clock, so each vault plays out the same way every time.
  3. Laser emitters flicker one beat before they fire.
  4. Reach the airlock before the oxygen gauge empties; collect key cards to open force-field doors.

Controls: Move: arrows or WASD, or swipe (touch: D-pad) · Restart the vault: Space twice or ⟲ (costs a life).

💡 Clear a vault within half a second of its par time for the Speed of Light badge.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Vault of Stars is game 30 of 42 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 5 · Beyond (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.

The Legends secret

Vault of Stars is Zone 5 · Beyond's Legends game. Start at vault 1 and clear all 15 without losing a life. Something is waiting beyond vault 15… Win the egg to enter the Hall of Legends — and all 5 Golden Eggs together win the Golden Crown.

“Legends say something deep inside the moon is still sending a signal…”

Badges in Vault of Stars

  • 👩‍🚀 First Contact — Clear vault 1
  • ✦ Relic Hunter — Collect every crystal in a vault
  • ⏱️ Speed of Light — Clear a vault from vault 2 on within half a second of its par time
  • 🌀 Zero-G — Drift 8 tiles in one go
  • 🔦 Blackout — Clear a dark vault without losing a life in it
  • 💨 Hull Breach — Reach the airlock of the Core Chamber after the hull breaches
  • 💎 Cache Raider — Grab 40 crystals in one relic cache
  • 🔭 Deep Space — Reach vault 10
  • 🔒 1 secret badge — keep playing…

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Vault of Stars unlocks a golden character: the Golden Astronaut.
