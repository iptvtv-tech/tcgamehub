Snake Escape Puzzle

Snake puzzle game — A knot of snakes, every one in another’s way. Tap a snake to slide it until it is blocked and clear the board in as few moves as you can. Free puzzle.

Puzzle Snake Expert · Zone 2 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Snake Escape Puzzle

  1. Tap or click a snake to slide it in the direction it faces.
  2. A snake moves until it hits another snake, a rock or the edge.
  3. Free every snake from the board before you run out of moves.
  4. Press R or ↺ to restart the board at no cost.

Controls: Tap / click a snake to slide it · R or ↺ to restart the board (free) · arrow keys + Space.

💡 Clear a board in the fewest possible moves for the Perfect Solve badge.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Snake Escape Puzzle is game 7 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 2 · Hard Mode (Expert difficulty). The zone opens once you have played every game in Zone 1.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Snake Escape Puzzle challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Heist Planner, the zone's Legends game.

  • 🪢 Giant Knot — Untangle a 9×9 puzzle
  • 🐍 Stampede — Free 20 snakes in one bonus round
  • 🔥 Snake Charmer — Reach a x4 combo
  • 🏅 Knot Master — Reach level 10
