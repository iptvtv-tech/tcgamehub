Neon Snake

Snake game — Classic snake with a neon glow: eat fruit, grow longer and dodge walls and portals as the pace climbs. Free, no download, levels and high scores.

Arcade Snake Action Easy · Zone 1 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Neon Snake

  1. Steer the snake with the arrow keys or WASD, or swipe on a phone.
  2. Eat the fruit to grow and to fill the level bar.
  3. Walls and obstacles appear from level 4 — from then on the edges no longer wrap.
  4. Eat quickly in a row to build a combo multiplier.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD · swipe on touch screens.

💡 Golden fruit is worth far more, and portals are a shortcut when you are cornered.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Neon Snake is game 1 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 1 · Arcade Classics (Easy difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Neon Snake challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in The Vault Job, the zone's Legends game.

Badges in Neon Snake

  • 🌟 Golden Bite — Eat a golden fruit
  • 🔥 Hungry Streak — Reach a x3 combo
  • 🌀 Wormhole — Travel through a portal
  • 💎 Gem Feast — Eat 15 gems in one bonus round
  • 🐍 Serpent Sage — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Five Grand — Score 5,000 in one run

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Neon Snake unlocks a golden character: the Golden Snake.
