Dock Master

Space traffic control game — Ships warp in from the void — drag each one a flight path to the docking bay of its colour. Keep the routes from crossing, dock the quota, and never let two hulls touch. Free.

Space Arcade Puzzle Ultra · Zone 5 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Dock Master

  1. Press on a ship and drag a line to the bay of the same colour (each colour has its own hull shape, shown on the bay pad too).
  2. The route commits the moment your line reaches the right bay — the ship flies it on its own, a little faster than it drifts.
  3. Two ships touching is a collision and costs a life. Redraw a route any time by dragging from the ship again.
  4. Dock the level’s quota to finish. From level 8 the bays trade colours on a timer — watch for the blink and reroute.

Controls: Drag a line from a ship to the bay of the same colour.

💡 Routed ships fly faster than drifting ones — routing a ship out of danger is often better than routing it home.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Dock Master challenge.
