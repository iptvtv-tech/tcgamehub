Station Defense

Space tower defense game — A free space tower defense game. Ships pour out of warp gates and fly a glowing lane to your station: build lasers, missile pods, cryo beams and tesla coils on the pads beside it, upgrade them, and fire the station cannon yourself. Short levels, a new lane every time.

Space Action Ultra · Zone 5 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Station Defense

  1. Tap or click a glowing pad to build a turret. Each costs ⚡ credits; every ship you destroy earns more. Missile Pods open at level 3, Cryo Beams at 6, Tesla Coils at 8.
  2. Tap a turret to upgrade it (two upgrades each) or sell it back.
  3. Tap anywhere else to fire the station cannon. It hits everything in a small blast and reloads in under a second.
  4. Ships that reach the station knock out its shields. Lose all ten and you lose a life. Press NEXT (or Space) to call a wave early for bonus points and credits.

Controls: Tap or click a pad to build, a turret to upgrade or sell · tap anywhere else to fire · Space: next wave · keys: hover a pad, 1–4 build, U upgrade, X sell.

💡 Pads between two stretches of the lane cover both, so build there first. Tanks shrug off lasers; lightning and missiles go straight through their armour.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Station Defense is game 27 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 5 · Beyond (Ultra difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Station Defense challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in Vault of Stars, the zone's Legends game.
