Prism Breaker

Brick breaker game — A rainbow brick-breaker: bounce the ball off your paddle, smash bricks, chain combos and catch multi-ball and explosive power-ups. Free to play.

Arcade Action Medium · Zone 1 Free · no sign-up · no download

How to play Prism Breaker

  1. Move the paddle with the mouse, by dragging, or with ← and →.
  2. Press Space or tap to launch the ball.
  3. Clear every breakable brick to finish the level.
  4. Catch falling capsules for multi-ball, a wider paddle and more.

Controls: Mouse / drag / ← → to move · Space or tap to launch.

💡 Hit the ball with the edge of the paddle to change its angle.

Press P to pause and M to mute. Works on desktop, tablet and phone.

Levels, bonus rounds and scoring

Prism Breaker is game 3 of 30 on Quick Play Arcade, in Zone 1 · Arcade Classics (Medium difficulty). Every game is open to everyone; the zones are the recommended order, and playing all six games of a zone completes it.

  • Every level is a little faster than the last, with something new every few levels.
  • Every 5th level is a bonus round: no way to lose, a timer and lots of points. Clear it to unlock a checkpoint you can start from next time.
  • Chain hits quickly to raise your combo multiplier.
  • Make the top 20 and type a nickname to go on the public leaderboard — no account needed. Boards reset daily and weekly.
  • One game a day is the Daily Challenge: the same layout for everyone, with its own board. Play today's Prism Breaker challenge.

Looking for this zone's Golden Egg? It is hidden in The Vault Job, the zone's Legends game.

Badges in Prism Breaker

  • ⛓️ Chain Reaction — Break 10 bricks without touching the paddle
  • 🔮 Seeing Triple — Catch a multi-ball
  • 💥 Kaboom — Set off an explosive brick
  • ✨ Flawless — Clear level 3 or higher without losing a life
  • 🌈 Prism Master — Reach level 10
  • 🏅 Ten Grand — Score 10,000 in one run

Win the zone's Golden Egg and Prism Breaker unlocks a golden character: the Golden Paddle & Ball.
